1 — The sheet-title link's tap area hangs over the text below it

The title link opens Google Sheets in a new tab. Its 44.8px tap area is centered on the title, so ~8px of it covers the top of the info line underneath — a tap on that line's upper half opens Sheets instead of doing nothing. The fix moves the extra tap area upward into the card's empty padding, leaving the info line alone.
