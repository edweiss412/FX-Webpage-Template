## 1.1 Resolved scope — do not relitigate

- **The three shapes are fixed by the originating spec** (`docs/superpowers/specs/ci/2026-08-09-round-economy-followups-2.md` §3.2, ratified): (a) script-constant parity, (b) sibling-list cardinality, (c) quoted-template quantity drift. No fourth shape; no narrowing to fewer than three (a shape may ship as advisory with a documented FP bound, but it ships).
- **Advisory-first is the ratified severity** (originating spec: "Advisory-first is acceptable"). Nothing in this arc emits a HARD finding. Promotion to hard is a later arc's decision with its own evidence.
- **Exclusion boundaries are normative, not tunable, and operationally LINE-BASED (R2 F4 — DocModel has no paragraph model, so the rule names what the code can see):** a number is excluded when (i) it sits in a fenced block, (ii) a dated qualifier phrase ("at plan time", "at authoring time") follows it within 40 characters on its line AND it is the NEAREST number preceding that qualifier — a qualifier excludes exactly one number, its closest predecessor (R6 F1: without nearest-binding, `all 37 sites (36 at plan time)` excluded BOTH; under nearest-binding the qualifier's closest predecessor is 36, and 37 compares), or (iii) its LINE contains an ISO date (dated historical records — probe transcripts, execution records — carry their dates by this corpus's own convention; historical measurements are never corrected). Residual: an UNDATED historical line can still draw an advisory — one glance, documented limit. (Fenced-region exclusion is already how `checkNumerics` walks the DocModel — `lib/specLint/numerics.ts:44`.)
- **The calibration numbers in §3 are the draft-time corpus measurement** (probe-before-argue for detector surfaces). Claims about corpus behavior are settled by re-running the probe, not argument.
- No UI surface; **impeccable-gate: N/A — no UI surface**.

## 2. Where the arms live

All three extend `lib/specLint/numerics.ts` beside `NUMERIC_NOUN_MISMATCH` (`lib/specLint/numerics.ts:88`), reusing the existing `DocModel` plumbing (`lib/specLint/parse.ts`) and the `Finding` shape, surfaced through `scripts/spec-lint.ts` like every other check. **Cross-file reads (R1 F4): shape (a)'s script text arrives through the EXISTING I/O boundary — `runLint`'s injected `FileResolver` (all I/O stays in the adapter, `scripts/spec-lint.ts:1`) resolves any same-line-named `scripts/` path the doc mentions and passes `{path → text}` into `checkNumerics` as a new optional argument; `numerics.ts` itself performs no I/O.** A path the resolver cannot serve (untracked, outside the contained root) contributes nothing and the occurrence is skipped silently (tripwire posture). ## 3. Corpus calibration — three layers, resolved after five rounds on this one vector (R5 comprehensive re-analysis)

**Layer 1 — the CONTRACT (normative).** The shipped arms implement the accept-sets, gates, and the three-part exclusion rule AS STATED IN THIS SPEC (fence / per-number 40-char qualifier / ISO-dated line). The spec is the authority on behavior; nothing below overrides it.

**Layer 2 — the INSTRUMENT (measurement record, not the contract).** The committed prototype (`docs/superpowers/specs/probes/2026-08-10-prose-count-probe-v5.ts`, path-parameterized) and its records (`docs/superpowers/specs/probes/2026-08-10-prose-count-probe-v5.report.txt`, `docs/superpowers/specs/probes/2026-08-10-prose-count-v5-shapeB-T6.txt`) are the dated calibration instrument whose measurements motivated the gate choices. The record holds EXACTLY: the shape-(b) tier 4-6 ladder rows, the tier-6 survivor listing, and the shape-(c) group listing — nothing else is claimed for it (R5 F4; the earlier "full ladder"/"complete record" language was wrong; the script's raw `v5-*` output basenames were committed under dated names, and re-running writes the `v5-*` names beside them). **The instrument DIVERGES from the contract in measured, enumerated ways, and the contract wins in each (R5 F2/F3):** it applies only the fence exclusion — the contract's qualifier + ISO-dated-line exclusions remove the 11 wrong advisories the R5 probe located among its tier-6 survivors; its shape-(c) run carries an unratified `RECORD` denylist — the contract drops it, and the 11 additional live qualifying groups the R5 probe exposed JOIN the population; its shape-(c) grouping is greedy-anchor — the contract is ALL-PAIRS within-doc (the R5 probe found 3 qualifying pairs the greedy grouping dropped, listed in the round record).

**Layer 3 — the arc's OWN record (AC-1, rebound).** The implementing arc runs ITS recognizer — the contract's — over this corpus snapshot, commits its own survivor records, and AC-3's classifications run over THOSE. No obligation to reproduce the instrument's populations exists (R5 F5 resolved: the instrument's 707-708 figure is background, not a bound; the contract's gates are final at ship time, with the classification informing severity copy and documented limits, never silently changing the accept-set post-hoc).

The recognizer's number-word list is stated by the contract as: the digit forms plus the number-words the instrument parses (committed in the script — R4 F3's point stands that prose must not restate the list).

### 3.1 Shape (a) — script-constant parity

The accept-set is deliberately keyed on structure: a doc names a script under `scripts/` by path or basename; that script contains a module-local `const <IDENT> = <integer>` with `IDENT` matching `/^EXPECTED_[A-Z0-9_]+$/`; the arm reads the declaration TEXTUALLY (never imports — the originating spec's own boundary). **Association is SAME-LINE plus NOUN-MATCH (R1 F2/F3, R3 F1): a cardinality qualifies when the script's name appears on the same line AND the cardinality's following noun singularizes to the noun derived from the constant's identifier** (`EXPECTED_SITE_TOTAL` → `site`; the derivation strips the `EXPECTED_` prefix and a trailing `_TOTAL`/`_COUNT`, lowercases, and singularizes) — without the noun predicate, unrelated same-line cardinalities (`4 re-runs` on the plan's :22, `18 files` at the spec's :327) would draw false advisories, which is exactly what the R3 probe showed. Multiple `EXPECTED_*` constants in one script each associate through their own derived noun; a constant whose derived noun matches no cardinality contributes nothing (tripwire posture). `DocModel` has no paragraph or table-row model, so same-line is the whole association scope. **Exclusion is per-NUMBER, not per-line (R1 F3):** a number is excluded when a dated qualifier phrase ("at plan time", "at authoring time") follows it within the same clause (within 40 characters), so `all 37 sites (36 at plan time)` compares the 37 and excludes the 36 on one line. **Measured universe today: one qualifying constant** — `EXPECTED_SITE_TOTAL = 37` (`scripts/verify-cn-operand-parity.mjs:80`), named by eight live docs (R5 F6 corrected the count) — with **two qualifying same-line occurrences** (R2 F1 probe: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-07-classname-array-join-cn.md:22` and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-07-classname-array-join-cn.md:651` both name the script on the line; the plan's :21 does NOT and is therefore outside the accept-set), **both agreeing with the constant: zero live mismatches**. The mixed-line case (a qualifying count and a dated-qualifier-excluded count on ONE line) has no live same-line-qualifying instance, so its fixture is SYNTHETIC and labeled as such. Every disagreeing number elsewhere (33, 36) sits in dated historical/review-round records the exclusions carve out.

### 3.2 Shape (b) — sibling-list cardinality (the FP-dominated shape; gates are load-bearing)

A naive "N <noun> followed by a list" recognizer over the corpus produces **12,198 hits with 83% count mismatch** — overwhelmingly false positives (the following list is not the claimed enumeration). The probe measured a cumulative gate ladder; each row is hits surviving ALL gates so far. **Predicate provenance (R3 F2, re-based on the §3 layer model at R6 F2): the measured ladder's exact predicates are the instrument's — list adjacency applied before tier 0, the 1,151→926 gate is nested-list indentation, "last clause" implemented as last-recognized-cardinality, plain mismatch counter at every tier except the final row's stop-at-break. The CONTRACT adopts these predicate semantics as stated here, and the shipped arm implements the contract (including the exclusions the instrument lacks); AC-1 binds to the contract, not to reproducing the instrument's population.**

### 3.3 Shape (c) — quoted-template quantity drift

Corpus probe: **27 candidate groups** (near-identical repeated prose/indented runs within one doc at Jaccard ≥0.85; re-probed at ≥0.87 the same recognizer yields 21 — R3 F3 corrected the draft's mischaracterization of both the structure set and the threshold). **The R1 review probe showed the draft's stricter differs-solely-in-numerics gate would MISS the ratified motivating instance** — the wedge-remeasure counting rule and its list-item disposition template differ in more than digits and are not both indented blocks — so that gate is rejected (R1 F1). **Ship posture, re-dispositioned at R4 F1 after THREE probe-refuted designs** (differ-solely-in-numerics missed the anchor; line-level Jaccard is 0.0748 for the anchor pair, unboundable; the span redesign's own gate excludes the anchor's single-numeric-slot clauses, and the best span pair at any threshold is an unrelated clause): **the mechanical arm CANNOT capture the wedge-remeasure anchor boundedly, and stops claiming to.** Shape (c) ships for the population the committed record shows it CAN serve — within-doc near-identical repeated runs bearing numeric diffs, with the instrument's recognizer mechanics ADOPTED as contract bounds where load-bearing, ALL stated (R6 F3 + R7 F1): candidates are trimmed physical LINES of 40-400 characters; similarity is SET Jaccard over ASCII-alphanumeric tokens INCLUDING numeral tokens (the R7 probe measured the digit-exclusion variant admitting 155 extra live advisories — 27 groups vs 182 — so numeral participation is normative); quantity extraction for the numeric-diff gate is DIGIT-ONLY for this shape (number-words apply to shape (b) only); pairing is all-pairs within-doc at Jaccard >= 0.85 (the record's 27 groups on this worktree; e.g. the measured trajectory-line and SQL-template families) — as an advisory, with the shipped recognizer's survivor set classified per AC-3. The wedge-remeasure instance itself is a **DOCUMENTED LIMIT of the mechanical arm**: it remains covered by the RULE half of the originating filing (the human numeric sweep), exactly the honest re-disposition pattern AGENTS.md ratifies for surfaces a mechanism cannot express (the step3 precedent). The three refuted designs are recorded here so no later round re-proposes them. ## 4. Guard conditions / accept-set discipline

- Inputs are markdown docs already parsed by `DocModel`; a doc with no qualifying structure produces zero findings from all three arms (opt-out by construction, matching the originating spec's "design and opt-in mechanics belong to the implementing arc" — the mechanics here are: always-on, advisory, structurally self-limiting).
- Everything outside each accept-set is IGNORED, never guessed at: an `EXPECTED_` const that is not a module-local integer literal, a list not adjacent to its claim, a repeated-run pair below the similarity threshold or whose diffs contain NO numeric change — all out, silently (R2 F2: a pair ABOVE the threshold whose diffs include a numeric change fires even when non-numeric text also differs; the wedge anchor itself sits below any bounded threshold and is the documented limit, R5 F1). The arms are drift TRIPWIRES, not census tools; silence means "no qualifying structure," not "verified consistent."
- Consequence bound: every finding is advisory with file:line + both numbers; a wrong flag costs one glance. Nothing rewrites, nothing blocks, nothing is silently wrong.

## 7. Documented limits

- Shape (b)'s FLAG rate after the full ladder is ~27% of structural hits (190 of 707-708 on this worktree); those flags MIX genuine drift with false positives, and the actual FP rate is unknown until AC-3's classification (R4 F4 — the earlier text mislabeled the flag rate as an FP rate). The arm stays advisory regardless. Adversarially constructed prose is out of scope (threat model: accidental drift by an ordinary author).
- Shape (a)'s universe is one script today; the arm is structural so the second `EXPECTED_` constant enrolls itself, but no claim is made about universes that do not yet exist.
- Cross-doc template drift (shape c is within-doc only) is out of scope per the originating spec.
- **The arms read two grammars they do not parse — CommonMark and JavaScript — and the bound they are held to is the LIVE CORPUS, not the grammars.** `docs/superpowers/specs/probes/2026-08-11-speclint-corpus-scan.ts` lints every tracked doc and commits every advisory to `…-corpus-scan.survivors.txt`; correctness is owed on those rows, and a false row there is a defect. A constructed document exercising a construct no tracked doc uses is a DOCUMENTED LIMIT, not a defect, and it is listed here rather than repaired. This is the same threat-model fence stated above, made checkable — thirteen consecutive whole-diff review rounds (R6, R8-R19) each repaired one more grammar corner under a bound quantified over all possible input, which is a bound a line-based recognizer cannot reach; the corpus bar is finite and closable. Known limits of this kind, all probed:
  - **Shape (b), list extent.** The counter reports only where the list's end is CERTAIN and REFUSES otherwise (returning no count, so no advisory): an unindented non-bullet line with the item's paragraph still open is CommonMark lazy continuation, an empty marker is an item or a setext underline depending on context, and neither is decidable by a line scan. Certain terminators are: end of document, a second blank line, anything after one blank line, a thematic break or fence short of the item's content column, an ATX heading, a blockquote, a marker of a different type, and the ratified checklist stop. Nesting is read from the nearest enclosing block above the list, which decides whether an outdented marker closes the list or continues it.
  - **Shape (a), script reading.** `readableScriptLines` is a hand-written lexical scan over strings, templates, comments and regex literals. On any construct it cannot resolve it returns `null` and the arm falls back to a NOUN-keyed duplicate-declaration refusal, so a lexer gap costs a tripwire rather than inventing one. The scan is not a JavaScript parser and is not held to being one.
