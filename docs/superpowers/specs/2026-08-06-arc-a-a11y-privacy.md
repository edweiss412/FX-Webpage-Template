### §2.1 BL-CREW-UNKNOWN-ASTERISK-TRAVEL-LEAK — withhold viewer-schedule dates at three sites

**Mechanism (exists; zero new plumbing).** `lib/crew/dateSuppression.ts` exports exactly one function, `suppressesDates(restriction: DateRestriction): boolean`, true iff `restriction.kind === "unknown_asterisk"`. `TravelSection` (`components/crew/sections/TravelSection.tsx`, single exported component) already computes `const ctx = resolveViewerContext(viewer, data)` and `ViewerContext` carries `dateRestriction: DateRestriction` (`lib/data/viewerContext.ts`, field sourced from `viewerCrew.dateRestriction`, fallback `{ kind: "none" }`). The fix imports `suppressesDates`, computes `const hideDates = suppressesDates(ctx.dateRestriction)` once, and gates the three sites. This is the same shape `ScheduleSection.tsx` and `TodaySection.tsx` already use (e.g. `TodaySection.tsx` hotel rows: `hideDates ? [{ k: "Hotel", v: firstHotel.hotel_name ?? "", span: 2 }] : [check-in/check-out rows]`).

**Site contracts (what renders under `hideDates`):**

- **Ground-transport leg dates** (inside `legs.map(...)`; guard var `dateIsPrimary = leg.date !== null`, drafting locators :409/:439). Under `hideDates`, `dateIsPrimary` is forced false, so `primary` falls through to the existing non-date arm `leg.time ?? leg.stage` — the same rendering a null-date leg already gets. The `<time dateTime={leg.date}>` element must not mount (the datetime attribute is itself the leak).
- **Hotel check-in/out** (gate `res.check_in !== null || res.check_out !== null`, drafting locator :514). Under `hideDates` the `<dl>` with Check in / Check out rows does not mount; the hotel card renders name and its other non-date fields exactly as it does when both fields are null. This mirrors the `TodaySection` hotel treatment (name survives, dates do not).
- **Personal flight dates — THREE render paths, all gated** (spec R1 F1: the site is the flight block, not one expression). (i) The structured expression `seg.date ? formatFlightDate(seg.date) : (seg.dateRaw ?? "")` (drafting locator :603): under `hideDates` BOTH arms are suppressed — `seg.dateRaw` is a raw date string and leaks identically — rendering the empty string. (ii) The Today/next-flight chip derived from `seg.date === flightTodayIso` (drafting locators :610-613): suppressed — "your flight is today" is exactly a viewer-schedule date claim. (iii) The RAW-FALLBACK row: a date-only segment fails `showStructured` (`hasContent` requires carrier/route/times, drafting locators :583-584) and renders `{seg.raw}` verbatim (drafting locator :642; `tests/components/crew/sections/TravelSection.flight.test.tsx` pins a `"3/22 Charter pending"` segment taking exactly this path). `seg.raw` is unparseable mixed text that routinely IS a date; under `hideDates` raw-fallback flight rows are WITHHELD entirely (conservative: the text cannot be split into date and non-date parts, and hiding is never silently wrong — §4 limit 7 records the content cost and trigger). **Card visibility derives from the VISIBLE-row set, not the unfiltered segments** (R2 F2: `showFlight`/`allHidden` are computed from unfiltered segments, drafting locators :328-331, and the card mounts from `showFlight`, :559-659 — withholding rows without re-deriving visibility would strand a silent empty "Your flight" card and suppress the section's empty state). ### §2.4 SHARELINK-CUE-VISIBILITY-1 — scroll the URL block to the cue

**Trigger and mechanism.** `ShareHub.tsx` holds `flash: number | null` (a nonce: bumped on token rotation via the render-phase `prevToken !== token` branch; cleared by timer `SHARE_LINK_FLASH_MS` and by the close/`linkActive` predicate). The cue element is the crew-URL `<code>` (`data-testid="admin-current-share-link-url"`, keyed by token, flagged `data-share-link-flash` while flash is non-null) inside the row `data-testid="admin-current-share-link-row"`, at the top of the popover scroller (`data-testid="share-hub-popover"`). New behaviour: **on every null→non-null and n→n+1 flash edge** (i.e. whenever `flash` changes to a non-null value — each rotation re-fires), an effect scrolls the URL row into view inside the popover scroller, using the repo's existing archive-scroll idiom (rAF + `querySelector` + `typeof target.scrollIntoView !== "function"` jsdom guard — the guard comment in `ShareHub.tsx` is the precedent, sibling instances in `PublishedReviewModal.tsx`, `CrewRowActions.tsx`, `ShowReviewSurface.tsx`). No ref exists on the URL row today; the effect resolves it by `querySelector` on the popover subtree (matching the archive idiom) or a new ref — plan pins one.

**Reduced-motion arm (ratified requirement).** Snapshot `window.matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)").matches` inside the effect (the repo's one-shot idiom — `GalleryLightbox.tsx`, `ReviewModalShell.tsx`; the `usePrefersReducedMotion` hook is for render-time params and returns `null` on first render, wrong for an event-time decision). Reduced motion: the scroll still happens but INSTANT (`behavior: "auto"`); default: `behavior: "smooth"`. Rationale: the cue's information ("the link changed, here it is") is the accessibility payload — skipping the scroll entirely would withhold it from exactly the users who asked for less motion; instant relocation delivers it without animation. Note the flash HIGHLIGHT under reduced motion is already ratified as "NO cue at all" (`DESIGN.md` §SHARE_LINK_FLASH_MS row) — that decision is untouched; the scroll is a separate, new surface and gets its own arm.

### Transition Inventory

1. **Cross-component live-region gate blindness stays.** The AST walk sees one file at a time; a parent-component gate hiding a child's region is invisible to it. Re-homed to the guard-file header on archive (§2.2); a future whole-program pass is a separate decision.

4. **The scroll cue's reduced-motion arm scrolls instantly rather than skipping** (§2.4 rationale). The flash highlight's separate no-cue-under-reduced-motion contract is untouched. Re-open trigger: user feedback that any auto-scroll is unwanted under reduced motion.
5. **Travel-date suppression covers the three named sites plus the four already-gated surfaces.** The dateSuppression header is the inventory of record; a NEW date-rendering surface must add itself there (review-time discipline, not a new guard — same posture as the existing header contract).
6. **Peer attendance stays visible to all viewers by owner ruling** (§1.1 item 1). Re-open trigger: the owner revisits the coordination-vs-privacy call.
7. **Raw-fallback flight rows are withheld entirely under `hideDates`** (§2.1 path iii). The non-date content a raw itinerary line may carry (e.g. "Charter pending") is hidden along with the date — the text cannot be split safely, and hiding is the conservative arm. The withholding is NEVER a silent empty card: card visibility re-derives from the visible-row set (§2.1 path iii), so an all-raw viewer gets the section's existing no-flight-data render, a designed state, not a stranded box. Re-open trigger: an `unknown_asterisk` crew member reporting missing itinerary context; the fix would be a parser-side split, not a render-side one.
8. **Sheet-authored free-text fields stay ungated** (`transportNotes`, `hotelAddress`, hotel `stayRows` values — §2.1 class-sweep ruling): prose fields are not date fields; a date an author types into one is editorial content, outside the marker's "which days YOU work is unconfirmed" semantics. Named in the `dateSuppression.ts` header. Re-open trigger: a real leak report through one of these fields.

9. **Under suppression the flight card renders only its SHAPE-CLOSED fields** — the route (airport codes) and the times. The carrier (`flightNo`/`airline`), the per-segment `conf`, and the itinerary-level `confirmation` are WITHHELD, because they are the parser's unvalidated remainder: it claims the date, route and times, then assigns whatever tokens are left to them. Cross-model diff review R3 probed three ordinary duplicated-date inputs that put a date there — `"2026-05-13 5/13 AA3002 …"` renders `Confirmation 2026-05-13`; `"EWR-FLL 2026-05-13 UNITED …05132026"` renders the ISO date inside the airline and `Conf 05132026`; `"5/13 MAY13 LGA - ORD …"` renders `MAY13` as the flight number — each escaping all five gated date paths. The rule is deliberately a WITHHOLD, not a date recognizer over those fields: enumerating date spellings does not terminate and widens the target every round, whereas "render only what cannot express a date" is closed. `flightNo` is in the withheld set precisely because its own shape (`^[A-Z]{1,3}\d{1,4}[A-Z]?$`) accepts `MAY13`. Content cost: a suppressed viewer loses the carrier name and confirmation code, keeping route and times — which is what a crew member reads off a phone. Re-open trigger: a crew member reporting they cannot identify a flight without the carrier.

11. **A date an author types into a ground leg's `stage` or `time` still reaches the viewer — RULED, not missed.** Cross-model diff review R4 probed it: a v4 date-like stage (`{stage:"5/13"}`) renders `Getting there 5/13`, and a v2 duplicated date cell parses to `{time:"5/14"}` and renders it. **Gating these was implemented and REVERTED**, and the reversal is the durable part. The closed-question rule that works for the flight card (limit 9 — can this field's own SHAPE express a date?) has no answer for either field, because neither has a closed shape. A `time` validator that accepts what sheet authors actually write must accept `1730`, `0800`, `noon`, `6p`, `8h00`, `8:00 PM PST`; enumerating those is the same non-terminating problem as enumerating date spellings, pointed the other way, and every miss SILENTLY DELETES a real call time — strictly worse than the leak it prevents (R5 audit probed nine such false rejects against the shipped-then-reverted regex). A `stage` allowlist would have to be the template vocabulary, and `STAGE_VOCAB` holds four entries while the 2026 template ships eight (`Pick Up Venue`, `Rental Pickup`, `Load at Warehouse`, …), so it would delete most real labels. These are therefore ruled exactly as limit 8 rules `transportNotes` and `hotelAddress`: an open-vocabulary author-typed field is editorial content, and a date in one is the author's writing, not the system asserting the viewer's schedule. Pinned by a test that asserts the RULING, so a future change gating them fails loudly and revisits the decision rather than the test. Re-open trigger: a real leak report, or a parser change that gives either field a closed vocabulary.

10. **A withheld raw flight row does not imply a date existed.** The section-level empty state names suppression as the reason only when a DATE was actually withheld — a dated hotel, a dated leg, or a segment carrying `date` or `dateRaw`. An undated raw row (`"Charter pending"`) is withheld by the raw-fallback rule with no date to hide, so the viewer gets the generic no-data copy rather than a false explanation. Reporting a plain absence beats asserting a wrong reason.
