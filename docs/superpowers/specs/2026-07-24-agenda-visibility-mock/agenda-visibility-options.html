One rule all three options share

Matching an agenda day to a calendar date is a best-effort read of the PDF — the date is parsed out of a heading like Tuesday, August 12, 2026, with a fall back to day order when no headings carry dates. On the shows where neither works, B and C cannot tell which day is his, so both fall back to showing every day (option A's screen). Nobody ever loses the agenda because the match failed.
