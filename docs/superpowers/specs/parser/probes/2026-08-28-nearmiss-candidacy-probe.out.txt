  Spec round 2 finding 1: the census proves a predicate classifies blocks. It does
  NOT prove `detectFieldNearMisses` gates emissions through that predicate. An
  implementation could expose a correct predicate for the census and separately
  hardcode suppression of the three known keys in the detector, satisfying every
  other criterion.

  near-miss that is NOT one of the three known keys, run the real detector over
