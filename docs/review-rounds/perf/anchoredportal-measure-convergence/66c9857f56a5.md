# perf/anchoredportal-measure-convergence — review-round filing

## spec — 3 rounds

**Examined:** the converged measure count for `components/admin/AnchoredPortal.tsx`,
the live probe that measures it, and the plan that ships it. Rounds 1, 2 and 3
returned BLOCKING with 4, 4 and 5 findings. All thirteen were accepted; none was
disputed.

**Judgment:** four findings were design-domain calls no mechanism would have
produced, and two corrected the ORCHESTRATOR's ruling as much as the author's
draft.

- The floor. The spec argued 2 was FORCED after killing both
  `IntersectionObserver` forms. Review produced `MutationObserver`: a reorder
  emits a `childList` record, the placement follow-up mutates only the portal's
  own style and is filterable without measuring, and its callbacks are
  microtasks, so unlike IO they run before paint. Killing one family had been
  accepted as a claim quantified over all mechanisms. Re-labelled: 2 is converged
  under the CURRENT notification mechanism, and MO is fenced as not taken for
  scope rather than refuted.
- The count's scope. "Exactly twice" carried neither of its qualifiers. A
  browser's initial `ResizeObserver` delivery schedules a further measure; jsdom's
  is a no-op stub. The figure counts commit-driven measures in jsdom.
- Registry expressibility, settled by probe rather than argument — and the first
  framing of the answer was itself too strong.
- Cap correctness: a non-binding wrong `maxHeight` preserved geometry and escaped
  every assertion. Repaired rather than documented, by asserting that a panel
  which FITS carries no cap, with the fit as an executable premise.

**Mechanizable:** nine findings were ONE class — an assertion that does not
discriminate what it claims — recurring across all three rounds. Round 3 stopped
the per-instance patching and derived the cover instead.

The class, and what a mechanism could catch:

- A premise comparing against a literal its own key function can no longer
  produce (the origin premise after the key grew to four members). Comparable to
  a lint over literal-versus-producer shape.
- A repair that DELETES coverage while appearing to improve. The round-2 repair
  rewrote a tail block and silently dropped `placements.length === 2`, so INV-4
  stopped asserting its own subject. Mechanizable as an assertion-count diff
  across a block replacement — the diff reads as progress, which is what makes it
  the worst-shaped edit.
- A gate that greps the FILE containing a check instead of running the check. A
  comment holding the id satisfied it.
- A claim of execution written ahead of the execution, twice in two rounds. Not
  fixable by more diligence; fixed by generating the mutant table FROM run output.
- Internal numbering drift and restructure residue (duplicated instruction block,
  stale task ordinal, table arity), all mechanically produced by collapsing five
  tasks to two, and all caught by a three-check sweep validated against known
  positives.
- A hand-authored citation count that stopped tracking its source (15 claimed, 22
  occurrences and 19 distinct present). Derived now, or dropped.

declined: process-facing under the 2026-08-25 mint freeze, and each candidate is
at one arc rather than the two the 2026-08-27 lift requires. Their done
conditions are properties of a guard — "the lint sees literal-versus-producer
drift", "the differ counts assertions across a block replacement" — not numbers a
product arc or a human would notice moving. Re-file trigger: a second arc hits
the same shape, or a product-facing arc is measurably blocked by one.
