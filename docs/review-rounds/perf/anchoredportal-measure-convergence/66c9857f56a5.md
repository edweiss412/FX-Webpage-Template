**A fifth mechanizable shape, and the fleet hit it four times today: A DERIVED
VALUE QUOTED IN PROSE.** `docs/agents/writing-plans.md` says "53 of the 113

**It took THREE re-measured counts, not one, and the first repair looked
complete.** The walk went 113 to 114, which fixed the `enrolled` label — and
AC-11 still failed, on `declined`. A plan that DECLARES its own acceptance

quotes, not the first one that reds.

Causation was proved rather than assumed: this branch changes neither
