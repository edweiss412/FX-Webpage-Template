## diff — 4 rounds

**Examined:** the live-region announce class across `app/` and `components/`
(twenty-odd surfaces plus its structural guard), seven freshness projections in
`components/admin/review/sectionFreshness.ts` and their probe harness, the
`unknown_asterisk` date-suppression predicate, the `RESYNC_QUALITY_REGRESSED`
action re-target and its new §12.4 parity guard, and the /help crosswalk's
short-label tier.

**Every round found something, and the count did not decay** — 4 BLOCKING-class
rounds, no taper. That is the pattern the convergence guidance says to distrust,
and it was earned: the findings were not one class dripped, they were a
succession of *different* guards each failing the same way.

**Mechanizable:** Yes. Nearly every finding was a guard or a claim that **could
not fail**, and the sub-shapes are worth listing because they recur:

| Round | Surface | What could not fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `dateSuppression.ts` | a header claiming every date surface was gated; four were not, proved by RENDERING the page |
| 1 | hotels projection | narrowed to the date LABEL; `nightsBetween` reads the same raw fields, so a year-apart pair painted one label and 2195 vs 4 nights |
| 1 | live-region detector | a six-line regex lookback that missed the real defect it was written to catch |
| 1 | `archivedTabOffer` | `empty:hidden` — the fix's own tidy-up compiles to `display:none`, removing the region from the a11y tree |

| 4 | site registry | eleven slack slots — the counts were wrong a second time, and raw index was compared against a non-exempt count |

Four rules come out of it:

1. **A guard that passes on a clean tree has proved nothing.** Every detector
   here was written, shipped, and then shown by probe to miss ordinary authoring
   forms. What ended that was not another widening: it was planting every shape
   review had named as an executable self-test — seventeen positive, five
   negative — so the scanner's own claims are exercised rather than asserted.
   The rounds where a widening landed *without* a self-test are exactly the
   rounds that came back.

2. **A premise that validates something ADJACENT to the case is not a premise.**
   The freshness probe suite asserted "a venue edit paints" and then ran six
   cases whose own mutations were never checked — a mis-pathed pair produced
   identical renders and reported byte-identical as a PASS. Each case now proves
   its own two inputs differ in the ADAPTED data. Same shape, one layer out: the
   `waitFor(element exists)` readiness signals that a permanent-mounting change
   silently turned into no-ops.

3. **Counts written from estimation are wrong; counts derived from the thing
   they describe are not.** This file put wrong numbers in a registry TWICE —
   announce-call counts, then site counts — and both times the assertion caught
   it only because something else failed first. The registry now asserts its own
   exactness against the scanner, which is the same fail-by-default posture the
   corpus walk uses.

4. **The fix's own tidy-up is a defect site.** Two of the sharpest findings were
   in repairs, not in the original code: `empty:hidden` re-hid a region the fix
   had just exposed, and the channel migration created duplicate speech by
   leaving the old speaker in place. A repair changes the shape of the thing it
   repairs; reviewing only the original defect misses that.

**One finding was refuted rather than filed**, recorded so it stays settled: R4
added the global show date to the `unknown_asterisk` leak list. `***` means
"which days YOU work is unconfirmed", not "the show's dates are secret" — the
date is on the link the viewer arrived through. The privacy question is dates
implying the VIEWER's schedule, which is narrower than "any rendered date".

**What did not close, and why it is filed rather than fixed:** per-outcome
channel coverage inside the four exempted files, the scanner's aliasing and
constant-attribute false negatives, and cross-component region ownership. All
three are recognizer-widening against an open class, which the briefs'
convergence bound explicitly does not treat as the criterion for closing —
`BL-CHANNEL-ANNOUNCER-RESIDUAL-ROLE-STATUS`,
`BL-LIVE-REGION-AST-WALK-RESIDUE` and
`BL-CREW-UNKNOWN-ASTERISK-TRAVEL-LEAK` each name the work, the reason, and the
probe evidence.
