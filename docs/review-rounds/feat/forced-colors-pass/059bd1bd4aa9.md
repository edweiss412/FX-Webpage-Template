**Judgment:** the disposition itself. Whether a forced-colors collapse is a defect
depends on what else the component renders, and the answer lives in the JSX rather
than in the class strings: a check glyph replacing a number, words the same value
selects, a border width and an indent, filled against hollow marks, an open menu.
Two rounds corrected assignments a mechanical rule would have made the same way.

One rule DID generalise out of it and is now stated in the plan before its rows: a
surviving carrier must be VISUALLY PERCEIVABLE. Forced colors is used by sighted
people who need contrast, so `aria-current` on a nav link carries nothing for them,
and a carrier painted in a forced property does not survive however geometric it
looks. Two independent carrier audits reached the first half; only one reached the
second, and the two disagreed on a positioned rail indicator until the paint was
read. That disagreement is the argument for reading rather than pattern-matching.
