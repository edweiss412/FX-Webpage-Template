**Six rounds of a recognizer that never grew is the result worth recording.** The standing rule under
same-axis recurrence is that the repair direction is narrowing, and this stage is what that looks like
when it is followed from round 1: the arm ends with THREE codes, a closed input set, and nine declared
limits, and every round moved a claim out of the mechanism and into either a declaration or §5. The
count is high and the artifact is smaller than it was at round 1. What the count measures here is a
design being narrowed to what it can prove, not a reviewer's imagination.

**Design content reached zero at round 6.** Both of that round's findings are consistency defects: §3.4

item 7. The second one had a TWIN in the probe record (§5.1, same stale reference), found by the

The HIGH was a precision fix, not a mechanism: `(code, doc, line, token)` cannot express two survivors
on one line, which the live corpus has eight times over.

**The recurrence this arc paid for, and it is the arc's own subject.** Three number-drift repairs
