### What this arc would tell the next one

**The instrument is part of the artifact.** Six findings across both stages were defects in a scan,
an audit or a sweep rather than in the design — an exemption keyed by identifier SPELLING inside the
instrument built to detect exactly that; a detector with an undefined accept-set while advertising
fail-by-default; an audit whose population was a remembered list; a set-equality criterion whose
evidence measured cardinality. **Audit the instrument before the result**, and prefer a metamorphic
probe to reading it: a semantics-neutral rename moved one scan's count and settled in one command
what a careful re-read had missed.

**Derivation is right for a COVER and wrong for BOTH SIDES OF A COMPARISON.** Two sides derived from
one constant cannot disagree, so the check is vacuous however rigorous it reads. The form that keeps
both properties is *derive the requirement, author the witness, assert the coverage*.
