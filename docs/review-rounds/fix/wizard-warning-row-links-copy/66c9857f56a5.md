## spec — 4 rounds

**Examined:** four rounds at this base, all BLOCKING, 23 findings declared (10 / 4 / 6 / 3). Every one was accepted and repaired by class sweep in the same round; none was argued down. The subject is a design for row-level "Open in Sheet" anchors on `UNKNOWN_FIELD` warnings, derived from the exporter's own block pipeline, plus a region link for the hotel ambiguity codes and a copy split so no card names a control it lacks. The finding count fell every round except R3, and R4's three were one BLOCKING (a binding that checked the label but not the value) and two NEEDS-ATTENTION.

**Judgment:** two distinct causes, and the second is the one worth recording.

The first two rounds were fact-of-the-corpus errors on the author's side, each settled by a probe the author could have run before drafting. R1's largest finding was that `ria.xlsx` sits in the committed corpus while the spec claimed no RIA workbook existed; R2's was that the three dispatching rows live on the `FORM` and `3rd Level` tabs, not on `INFO` and `GEAR`, because the markdown fixture the author probed concatenates every tab and hides which one a line came from. That second error changed the design: the tabs are outside the region allowlist, and a scanner that walked every tab would still have had its links collapsed to `#gid=0` by the read-time guard. The repair (scanner anchors carry a `scope`, the guard applies to unscoped anchors only) is the right shape, but the spec-self-review rule that would have found it in five minutes is "probe the workbook, not the markdown", which is the general form of "probe-before-argue" that `docs/agents/spec-self-review.md` already states for detector surfaces. The author read it as a rule about detectors and not about provenance.

Rounds 3 and 4 were the convergent tail of one guard, T1, which pins the equivalence the whole design rests on. R2 said it compared content but not coordinates; R3 said the coordinate check as written could not pass on the corpus because a literal pipe inside a cell fractures the row for the parser; R4 said the repaired check bound the coordinate to the label alone and so admitted a wrong cell wherever a label repeats with a different value, and the corpus has two such pairs. Each of the three was a real hole and each repair was one clause. The rate did not decay because the guard was being specified one predicate at a time, in prose, against a corpus the reviewer was probing and the author was not. The R4 form, coordinate bound to the full `(label, value)` join key through the shipped conversion, is the one the author should have written at R2 by asking "what is the join key" rather than "what does the reviewer name".

**Mechanizable:** none filed. Process mint freeze in force; the observation below stays here as a named shape.
