# Review-round filing: fix/sync-log-show-id-duration @ d2a31e4aa021

## spec — 5 rounds

**Examined:** whether four spec rounds on a ~15-line production change bought their cost, and what would have shortened the train.

**Judgment:** rounds 1 and 2 were worth their cost and then some. R1 finding 1 caught an application-level deadlock that would have hung the cron on every first-seen apply — the design passed a `shows` id that was still uncommitted inside the transaction awaiting the sink, so the FK check on the sink's separate connection would wait on a transaction waiting on it. That defect was invisible to inspection and the spec's own rationale had the causality backwards, citing the breaking case as the justification. R2 finding 5 caught a data-destruction hazard: `prune_sync_log` deletes every qualifying row in whatever database it targets, and the sink's resolver prefers `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, which on this machine is the shared validation project. Neither is the "guard does not pin what it claims" shape the round-economy retrospective identifies as the dominant late-round waste; both are real defects in shipping behavior.

**Mechanizable:** one class, and it is the reason there was an R3 at all. Three findings across two rounds were instances of a single vector — *a `sync_log` row that could name its show does not* (R1 F2 at the sink, R1 F7 in the accept-set, R2 F1 at seven callers). Each was repaired per-instance, which is precisely what `docs/agents/writing-plans.md` forbids once a class is nameable: *"the 3-round same-vector threshold is a ceiling, not a waiting period."* The class was nameable after R1 F2. The structural defense — a filesystem-walked meta-test requiring every `sync_log` call site to either pass a `drive_file_id` or hold a registry row — was designed only after R2, unprompted, and lands in the arc as `tests/sync/_metaSyncLogAttribution.test.ts`. Shipping it in the R1 repair would have made R2 F1 a red test instead of a review round.

Second mechanizable item: the bare-citation class. `pnpm spec:lint` flagged 51 malformed citations on the first run and 24 more after the R1 repair, both times because same-file shorthand (`` `:2425` ``) was reintroduced during editing. Both were swept mechanically in one pass. Running `spec:lint` before *each* repair commit, not only before dispatch, removes it entirely.

**Infra:** none. All three dispatches returned clean verdicts; no reaper kill, no `no_verdict`, no rollout scrape. The convergence gate blocked the R1 brief for stating its consequence bound in non-canonical wording — correctly, and the brief was rewritten rather than overridden, which improved it.

**Diagnosis of the residual:** the spec absorbed plan-stage material. Three of R2's six findings were about test assertions (per-sink oracle coverage, prune isolation, `cron.job.active`) — all real, all properly plan-stage. §5 had grown from an acceptance posture into an assertion-by-assertion table, and a spec that specifies assertions invites assertion-shaped findings at the stage where closing them costs a full round. The corrective is to move that content to the plan, which already carries it at higher fidelity, and leave the spec holding ACs, posture, principles, and the two hazards that are genuinely design-level because they can destroy data or mislead.

**R3 addendum.** Round 3 returned six more, and two changed the deliverable rather than the prose. F1 showed the seven onboarding caller sites are mutually exclusive branches, so no DB scenario can cover the class — which is the argument for the static meta-test, and the reason it is the oracle here rather than a nicety. F6 refuted a safety claim this spec had made outright: `_metaDestructiveDbTargetGuard` discovers only `reset_validation_data`, and `_metaLocalDbUrlGuard` scans only `LOCAL_TEST_DATABASE_URL`, so neither covers a prune test connected through `TEST_DATABASE_URL`. The spec had cited both as already-handling the hazard. Extending the destructive-target guard's discovery is now part of the deliverable.

That is the round-economy lesson of this arc in one line: the findings that cost the most rounds were the ones where the spec asserted a protection instead of shipping it.

**R4 addendum, and the structural response.** Round 4 returned five findings and **not one was about the design** — all five were assertion or guard completeness. That is the measurement this filing predicted after R3, so it was acted on rather than argued with: the spec's §5 assertion inventory moved to the plan, leaving posture, the failure each requirement prevents, and the hazards that are design-level because they can destroy data or return an empty result indistinguishable from the bug.

Two of the five changed the deliverable rather than the prose, and both are the same shape as R3 F6 — a control asserted rather than shipped:

- **F4** found a real hole in the structural defense added after R2. Its recognizer was hand-listed and omitted the two canonical exported writers, while `app/api/cron/sync/route.ts:4,21` imports and wires `writeSyncLog` directly. A plain call evaded both the recognizer and the registry with no obfuscation. The recognizer is now derived from the module's exports — the same "derive a cover, don't enumerate" rule the class-sweep discipline already required, applied to the guard that exists to enforce it.
- **F3** showed the guard extension promised after R3 was necessary but insufficient: `CALLS_LOCAL_GUARD` matches a call *whose name looks right*, without establishing that the guarded value is the URL handed to `postgres`, that the call precedes the connection, or that the callee is the real import.

**The pattern across all four rounds, stated once:** every finding that cost a round was a place where the document asserted a protection instead of shipping one — a rationale for explicit-wins that had the causality backwards (R1), a "complete" list that was not (R2, R3, R4), meta-guards cited as covering a hazard they never matched (R3), and a recognizer enumerating shapes instead of deriving them (R4). The prose was confident in exactly the places the mechanism was absent. What converged the arc was not more prose but three shipped mechanisms: a probe, a derived recognizer, and a CI-keyed anti-vacuity condition.
