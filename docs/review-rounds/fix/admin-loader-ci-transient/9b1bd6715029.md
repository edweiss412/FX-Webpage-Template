### What the rounds bought, and what they cost

observation that BLOCKING findings per round do not decay, and it is the reason the round cap exists.

What changed is where they came from. After round 2, six further defects were found by me rather
than by a reviewer — four by the mutation gate, two by planting an unscored module — and the
calibration lesson is uncomfortable and worth recording: the wrapper FELT risky, so it was

and held all four survivors. The code that feels too simple to plant is where the gate finds things.

The clearest signal that the repairs went the right direction is the wrapper's mutation site count:

each round is the shape the convergence rule warns about; a repair that deletes code is the shape it
asks for.
