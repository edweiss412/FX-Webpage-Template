**Mechanizable — and the mechanism is in the diff.** `scripts/mutation-weight-plant.mjs`
turns "the suite asserts X" into "a defect in X makes the suite red". It went 15 → 26
planted defects across these rounds, and it is the reason round 4 could be entered with
the class closed rather than narrowed. Three findings were caught by it BEFORE the
reviewer reached them, and three defects it exposed were in the SUITE rather than in the
code — including a tie-break fixture that passed under the exact defect it named, and a
median fixture using two values, for which mean and median are always equal.

**The generalisable rule, and it is cheap:** a suite covering an instrument whose output
feeds a spec is not finished when it passes. It is finished when a planted defect in
every claim it makes causes it to fail. Authoring the plant list beside the first
assertion would have moved most of rounds 2 and 3 into round 1.

**Judgment — not mechanizable, and settled by measurement rather than argument.** The
four-way design fork; the choice of the BINDING LEG over the spread as the criterion,
which the data forced when the spread got worse while the longest leg got shorter; and
the decision to EXCLUDE a surface no seed run has measured rather than synthesise a rate
for it.

**Round 4 is where the shape changed**, and it is worth recording why rather than
just that. Its four findings were all small and all of one kind — an assertion with no
plant behind it, two medians that picked the upper-middle value for an even population,
an acceptance criterion left carrying a bound the design section had already retracted,
and a line of report text contradicting the code beneath it. None touched the design.
The verdict moved from BLOCKING to NEEDS-ATTENTION on the same class of finding,
which is what convergence looks like here: the class did not disappear, its instances
got cheap.

The median defect is the one to generalise from. Three private copies existed and two
were wrong in the same direction, while the third — inside `seedRates` — was right. A
reviewer found the two wrong ones; nothing in the suite could, because the fixture for
one of them had a single-surface population where every statistic collapses to the same
number. **The repair was not to fix two call sites but to delete them: one exported
`median`, three callers, one plant.**

**Infra:** none. No round was lost to a wedge, a usage wall, or a `no_verdict`.
