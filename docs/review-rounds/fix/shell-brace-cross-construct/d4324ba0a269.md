**Judgment:** five calls, each recorded where a later reader will hit it rather than only here.

1. **The round-1 probe refused half of its own finding.** `>(` reaches a psql call that runs once,
   which is the forbidden direction; `<(` ran zero times and is a different case. Its regression row
   asserts delimiter accounting only, in place, in the test body.
2. **The repair direction was NARROWING, not parser growth.** The accept-set gained two openers the
   same `(`/`)` walk already knew how to close, and the complement stayed default-denied. Extending
   `doubleQuotedEnd` would have been the parser-growth move AND wrong on bash's own semantics, which
   is the useful part: the fence and the correct behaviour pointed the same way, and a fence is cheap
   to hold when they do.
3. **A probe erring in BOTH directions was narrowed rather than grown.** `depth.mts` was described as
   over-approximating, and over-counting really is the conservative direction for a bound — but
   `depthOf` ignores quotes while decrementing on a quoted `)` or `}`, and counts no
   process-substitution openers, so it can UNDER-count too: `echo ${OUT:-<(echo }; psql -c 'x')}`
   reports max 1 against a production matcher depth of 2. **A measurement that errs in both
   directions bounds nothing.** The claim moved to AC-6's measured same-session ratio, which does not
   depend on how `d` is counted, and the probe was demoted in writing to a REPORTER. Round 4 found
   the header comment still making the withdrawn claim — the same drip the sweep rule exists to
   stop, one file away from where round 2 fixed it.
4. **Round 3's gate pins the DESTINATION by full-record digest, not the field the finding named.** A
   candidate with the right `nested` and a wrong `offset` is still wrong, so the fix pins the whole
   record. The digest was derived by evaluating the probe's own `CASES` entry rather than retyped
   from the reviewer's message, and the gate was proven in BOTH directions: the real candidate
   passes, the wrong one reports `DESTINATION MISMATCH` and exits 1.
5. **Round 4's repair swapped whole literal TOKENS, quotes included.** The two crossed pairs used
   different quote styles, and unescaping then re-escaping bodies that carry deliberate `\\$`
   sequences is how a repair silently corrupts the thing it repairs. Swapping the token verbatim is
   valid JavaScript regardless of style and cannot mangle an escape.
